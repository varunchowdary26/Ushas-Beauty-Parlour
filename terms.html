Welcome to Ushas Herbal Beauty Parlour. By booking an appointment or using our website, you agree to the following terms and conditions governing our salon services.

1. Appointments and Arrival

  • Appointments can be scheduled online through our booking form or by calling the parlour directly.
  • Each time slot is reserved exclusively for one booking to ensure dedicated care.
  • Please arrive 5 to 10 minutes prior to your scheduled appointment time. Late arrivals may require rescheduling so that subsequent clients are not delayed.

2. Service Pricing and Payments

  • Service prices displayed on the website (such as starting rates for Hair Care, Facial & Cleanup, Bridal Makeup, Waxing & Threading, Pedicure & Manicure, and Head Massage & Spa) represent base rates.
  • Final pricing may vary depending on hair length, custom bridal packages, or additional herbal treatments requested during your visit.
  • Payment is collected at the parlour upon completion of your service.

3. Skin Sensitivities and Herbal Treatments

While we specialize in gentle herbal and natural beauty formulations, every individual's skin is unique. Please inform our beautician prior to treatment if you have any known allergies, skin sensitivities, or dermatological conditions.

4. Cancellations and Rescheduling

If you need to reschedule or cancel your appointment, please notify us by phone at least 4 hours in advance. For bridal makeup bookings, advance notice of at least 48 hours is appreciated.

5. Authentic Feedback

Our Feedback Wall is intended for genuine clients of Ushas Herbal Beauty Parlour. Submissions must be respectful, truthful, and based on real service experiences.
